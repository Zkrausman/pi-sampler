# Evidence-first Fix/Harden hindsight model

The minimal hindsight product is now centered on two commands: `/conversation-catalog` and `/hindsight-document`. The report places cited snippets beside direct-evidence strengths and inference lessons, and shows matching model-proposed **Harden** or **Fix** actions linked by shared evidence references. A Harden proposal preserves demonstrated good behavior with a concrete guardrail, template, test, or checklist; a Fix proposal corrects a demonstrated risk. Flow/map exports were removed as speculative drill-downs. Related: [[sources/obs-2026-08-12-evidence-first-hindsight-pr-opened]].
