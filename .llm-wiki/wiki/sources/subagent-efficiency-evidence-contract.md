# Subagent efficiency evidence contract

Subagent hindsight analysis must distinguish evidence of delegation from evidence of delivery. A report only assesses explicitly recorded exact `subagent` calls with matching same-ID results. Delivery quality is always an inference and requires citations to a result and its own immediate chronological follow-up, bound through an opaque redaction-safe pair marker. This prevents lone-call claims and cross-delegation evidence mixing. Findings link to model-proposed Fix/Harden actions by shared evidence.
